
# Your Shopify App Bill Is the Quietest Cost in the Store

Product costs are visible. Shipping is visible. The app line is a dozen small charges that renew on different days and never get reviewed.

A store doing $20k a month commonly carries $400–900 in apps. A third of that duplicates features Shopify shipped for free in the last two years.

## Why the bill grows

- **Trial-to-paid drift.** A two-week trial solves one problem, then stays forever.
- **Overlap.** Three apps touch email; two touch reviews; two touch translation.
- **Seat creep.** Per-seat pricing grows with the team, not with revenue.
- **Usage tiers.** A plan that was $29 at 500 orders silently becomes $79 at 2,000.

## The 30-minute audit

**1. Export the charges.** Settings → Billing → Bills, last 90 days. One column per app per month.

**2. One line per app.** Name, monthly cost, the single job it does, and the number it moves (conversion, AOV, support time, page speed).

**3. Mark the overlaps.** Anything two apps do, one of them is a candidate.

**4. Check the native list first.** Before keeping an app, check whether the platform now ships that feature. Discounts, bundles, reviews, translation and email have all moved into the core in recent releases.

**5. Cut in one pass, not gradually.** Cancel the duplicates and the $0-impact apps today; keep a note of what breaks.

**6. Re-measure in 30 days.** If conversion or speed did not move, the app was decoration. If it did, you learned which number to defend next time.

## What to keep

- The app that owns your **checkout money path** (payments, tax, subscriptions).
- The app that produces a **number you check weekly**.
- The one that prevents a **legal problem** in your market.

Everything else is a candidate.

## A worked example

A 6-figure store with 19 apps:

| Category | Apps | Monthly | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reviews | 2 | $84 | $0 (native) |
| Email/SMS | 2 | $120 | $59 (one) |
| Translation | 2 | $70 | $35 (one) |
| Upsell | 3 | $96 | $32 (one) |
| Rest | 10 | $210 | $180 |
| **Total** | **19** | **$580** | **$306** |

Same storefront, same conversion. The saving is $274 a month, or $3,288 a year, for one afternoon.
