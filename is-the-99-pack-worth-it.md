
# Is a $9.99 AI Company Pack Worth It? What You Get vs Free

Short answer: it is worth it if you already pay for an agent and still write every prompt from scratch. It is not worth it if you have not decided what the agent should produce.

Here is the honest comparison, including the part where you should keep your money.

## What is free, and good

- **The 8-file starter** on [GitHub](https://github.com/psorii/one-person-ai-company-starter): workspace rules, seven approval gates, idea scoring, a brief and a status example. That is enough to test whether the structure fits you.
- **This site's guides**: the approval gate list, the CLAUDE.md template, the status format. No email required.
- **The score tool**: paste your file, get 12 checks and the three things to fix first.

If those three get you moving, stop. The pack is not a requirement.

The difference is not information. It is **decisions already made**: which roles exist, what each one owns, what a week looks like, what gets measured.

## The arithmetic most people skip

If the pack saves you one hour of setup a month, at any rate above $10/hour it pays for itself. If it stops one agent from publishing a wrong price or emailing a customer with a broken claim, it pays for a year.

The honest counter-argument: a disciplined person can write all 39 files from the free guides in a weekend. If that is you, do that instead. The pack is for people whose weekend is already full.

## When you should not buy

- You cannot name the file your agent should produce. Fix that first; the free starter has the list.
- You only want prompts. Prompts are the least valuable part and are free everywhere.
- You need a hosted product. This is a folder, not software.
